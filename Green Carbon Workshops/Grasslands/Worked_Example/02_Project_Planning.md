## What this page needs to carry

Enough for a reader to follow the same five steps on their own site, in the same order Part 2
presents them:

| Step | What to show |
|---|---|
| **1 — Study area** | The site area, the boundary rule in one sentence, what was excluded and why, and whether each included unit is native, seeded or restored |
| **2 — Stratify** | Each stratum's mapped rule, its area in m², its restoration and burn history, the expected source of variation, and whether it is a reporting unit, a comparison unit, or both |
| **3 — Pools** | Which pools were included and excluded, with the reason for each; the tree decision; the full-profile target, reporting windows and depth increments |
| **4 — Sample size** | Area per stratum, confidence level, soil and root CV **with their sources**, the separate precision targets, the calculated sizes, the rounding and minimum rule applied, the final field count, and the root subsample count |
| **5 — Locations** | The per-stratum allocation, an anonymized map or illustrative coordinates, the sampling strategy, the plot layout, the permanent/single-use decision, and the core-offset rule |

Then the thing worth more than any of the above: **what the division actually bought** — the
within-stratum spread against the pooled spread, which is the argument for stratifying made with
the project's own numbers rather than in the abstract.
